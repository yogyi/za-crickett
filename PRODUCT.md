# ZA Cricket

## Register

Brand — marketing storefront and brand site for a cricket equipment company. Design communicates product and ambition; commerce is secondary to trust and identity.

## Users & purpose

Singapore- and Asia-based club, school, and pathway players shopping English willow bats, protection, and bat-care bundles. Parents and coaches buying kit. Athletes evaluating sponsorship-grade gear.

Purpose: sell premium cricket equipment with clear retail pricing, custom Signature bat orders, and direct team contact.

## Brand personality

Ambitious · disciplined · athlete-proven · Singapore-rooted · performance-first.

Voice: confident and direct, never hype-for-hype. Purple brand (#4B2E63) as committed identity — not soft cream or generic SaaS blue.

## Anti-references

Generic Shopify templates, purple-on-white AI landing kits, cream/terracotta “editorial” defaults, dense newspaper layouts, emoji-heavy sports marketing.

## Design principles

1. Brand purple and real product/athlete imagery carry the first viewport.
2. One job per section; avoid card clusters when a clear composition works.
3. Mobile-first commerce flows with large tap targets.
4. Motion is present but purposeful (hover, gallery, hero) — never noisy.
5. Preserve existing type and token system; extend, don’t reinvent.
